C6 Zigbee Stick

Turns an ordinary ESP32-C6 board into a Zigbee coordinator for Home Assistant. The firmware installs from your browser, and the integration finds the stick and sets up Zigbee2MQTT for you.

1. Flash the board

Connect the ESP32-C6 to your computer with a data USB cable (to the USB connector, not UART) and press the button. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Your browser cannot talk to USB ports. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. Your browser cannot talk to USB ports. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer.

Firmware: esp-coordinator 1.5.80 (ZBOSS). The board's flash will be fully erased.

2. Add the integration to Home Assistant

Once, through HACS: “HACS → ⋮ → Custom repositories”, URL https://github.com/f1devbin/c6-zigbee-stick, type “Integration”. Install “C6 Zigbee Stick” and restart Home Assistant.

3. Plug the stick in

Connect the board to the Home Assistant machine. A “Discovered: C6 Zigbee Stick” notice appears. Press “Configure”: the integration installs and starts Zigbee2MQTT. Then add Zigbee devices in Zigbee2MQTT with “Permit join”.

Details, limitations and source code: github.com/f1devbin/c6-zigbee-stick
